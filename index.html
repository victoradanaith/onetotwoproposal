The Big Picture

Why 1:2 Coffee can reach 500 branches without growing the head office — and what we build to make that true.
1:1

North Star

People stop being data-entry clerks and become reviewers and approvers. The robot drafts. The human decides.

1:2 grew on volume, discipline, and a five-person back office. Our program protects that ratio as the network multiplies: same team, less redundant workload — never less responsibility. We measure it in hours unloaded per person, and redirect the freed time to what robots can't do: coaching baristas, tasting quality, caring for customers.

DATA IS WHAT YOU OWN · OPERATIONS IS WHAT YOU DO · PERFORMANCE IS WHAT YOU MEASURE · ANALYTICS IS WHAT YOU DECIDE
1:2

Four-Layer Architecture

Four layers, three systems of truth, one AI capability across everything.

Thanvasu POS owns sales truth. TRCloud owns financial truth. Lark is the operational hub where every department works daily. Beneath them, a cleaned data foundation with one master ID per branch, vendor, product, and person; above them, a performance layer measuring every department — including hours unloaded — and the analytics that turn numbers into decisions.

Accounting Automation Status

The role shift is already happening in Accounting: from keying data to reviewing and approving. M1 reads landlord and utility bills automatically — including scans and photos — matches vendors, splits line items and withholding tax, and checks document completeness per branch. M3's reconciliation logic was rebuilt from the team's manual method and matched their results on every figure.
